Calculated fields used in the Tableau workbook "retail-sector-analysis"
Data source: data/clean/retail_financials_wide.csv (one row per company and year)

Retailer
CASE [Ticker]
WHEN 'WMT' THEN 'Walmart'
WHEN 'TGT' THEN 'Target'
WHEN 'COST' THEN 'Costco'
WHEN 'KR' THEN 'Kroger'
WHEN 'HD' THEN 'Home Depot'
WHEN 'LOW' THEN "Lowe's"
WHEN 'BBY' THEN 'Best Buy'
WHEN 'DG' THEN 'Dollar General'
END

Gross Margin
(SUM([Revenue]) - SUM([Cost Of Revenue])) / SUM([Revenue])

Operating Margin
SUM([Operating Income]) / SUM([Revenue])

Net Margin
SUM([Net Income]) / SUM([Revenue])

Capex % Revenue
SUM([Capex]) / SUM([Revenue])

Inventory Turnover
SUM([Cost Of Revenue]) / SUM([Inventory])

Days Of Inventory
365 / [Inventory Turnover]

Revenue Base 2019
{FIXED [Ticker] : SUM(IF [Calendar Year] = 2019 THEN [Revenue] END)}

Revenue Index
100 * SUM([Revenue]) / MAX([Revenue Base 2019])

Revenue Growth 2019 to 2025
SUM(IF [Calendar Year] = 2025 THEN [Revenue] END) / SUM(IF [Calendar Year] = 2019 THEN [Revenue] END) - 1

Margin Change 2019 to 2025 (points)
100 * (
  SUM(IF [Calendar Year] = 2025 THEN [Operating Income] END)
  / SUM(IF [Calendar Year] = 2025 THEN [Revenue] END)
  -
  SUM(IF [Calendar Year] = 2019 THEN [Operating Income] END)
  / SUM(IF [Calendar Year] = 2019 THEN [Revenue] END)
)

Notes
- Calendar Year labels fiscal years: year-ends in January to June count as the previous calendar year.
- Kroger's cost of revenue excludes depreciation and its inventory is derived (FIFO inventory minus LIFO reserve), so its gross margin and inventory turnover are the least comparable.
- Inventory turnover uses year-end inventory, not the yearly average.

Highlight Flag (only if you built the dropdown)
IF [Highlight retailer] = 'None' THEN [Retailer]
ELSEIF [Retailer] = [Highlight retailer] THEN [Retailer]
ELSE 'Others'
END
